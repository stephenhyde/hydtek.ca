FAQ

Who is it for?

Casual players who want to know what the openings are called and what they're trying to do, without sitting down to memorise 15-move lines. If you play online and want to recognise the Italian, the Sicilian or the London when you see them, it's for you.

Is it free?

Yes. Chess Openings: Learn & Quiz is free to download and use, and is supported by ads. There is no subscription.

When is it coming out?

Soon, for iPhone and iPad. This page will link to the App Store as soon as it's live.

Do I need an account?

No. You can learn and play without one. Signing in with Apple or Google saves your progress to your account so it comes back on a new device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
