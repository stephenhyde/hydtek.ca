TekTV

IPTV player for iPhone & iPad · Free

TekTV is a free IPTV player for iPhone and iPad that plays your own M3U playlists and Xtream Codes logins.

TekTV doesn't include any channels or content. You add your own playlist from your provider: an M3U link, an M3U file or an Xtream Codes login.

FAQ

What is TekTV?

TekTV is a free IPTV player for iPhone and iPad. It plays your own M3U playlists and Xtream Codes logins, with a TV guide grid, Movies & Series, favourites, a mini player, Picture in Picture and AirPlay.

Is TekTV free?

Yes. TekTV is free to download and use, and is supported by ads.

Does TekTV include channels?

No. TekTV does not include, sell or link to any channels or content. You add your own playlist from your provider.

Should I use an M3U link or an Xtream Codes login?

Both work. An M3U link is the simplest: paste one link to get live TV, plus the TV guide if the playlist includes one. If your provider gives you an Xtream Codes login, use it to also unlock the Movies and Series tabs, with Continue Watching and next-episode autoplay, and the TV guide loads automatically.

Does TekTV work on iPad?

Yes. TekTV is built for both iPhone and iPad, with a sidebar layout and a full TV guide grid on iPad.

Do I need an account? What happens to my data?

No account is needed. Your playlists and logins stay on your device, and you can delete everything at any time in Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
