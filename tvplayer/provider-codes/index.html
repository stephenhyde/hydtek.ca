Provider codes for TekTV

TekTV is a free IPTV player for iPhone and iPad. Licensed providers can get a short provider code (for example moose) for their customers to enter in TekTV, instead of typing a playlist link or server address.

How it works for your customers

  1. They open TekTV → Add Playlist → Provider Code, and enter your code.
  2. TekTV shows “Add your name?”. For M3U playlists the channels load straight away. For Xtream Codes, your service is selected and they sign in with their own username and password.

Who can get a code

Provider codes are only for providers that hold the distribution rights or licences for the channels and content they offer. To apply, we'll ask you to:

  • show evidence of those rights or licences;
  • sign a provider agreement with Hydtek Software Inc.;
  • agree that your name, code and playlist link or server address may be visible to TekTV users;
  • keep your playlist link or server address up to date.

We can remove a code at any time, and any complaint about rights means the code is removed immediately.

Apply

Email support@hydtek.ca with your company name, website, the type of service (M3U or Xtream Codes), and the code you'd like.

TekTV itself provides no channels or content.
